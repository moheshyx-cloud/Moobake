QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Where do you deliver?

Delivery zones are controlled by MooBake in the configuration. Ask the bakery if your address is covered.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Pickup is available only in Gothenburg, Sweden. The exact pickup address is provided by the bakery after the order is confirmed.

How far in advance should I order?

Preparation times are configurable by MooBake. Choose an available date at checkout and wait for confirmation.

Can I customize a cake?

Yes. Use the custom cake request form and describe what you want.

What about allergens?

Check each product's allergen information and tell MooBake about allergies in the order notes. Products may be prepared around common allergens.

Do I pay online?

No. This first version is an order-request website. It does not process card payments.
